How to play

Click a location to walk there and use it. Click ground to move (WASD works too) · Space or click your platform again to commit 1 Power at Council · R raven-vote from afar · scroll to zoom, M for the war-map.

Each Season: roam and gather. Mid-roam the Herald posts the next dilemma. When the horn sounds, race to the Council and bid Power — your first token locks your side. Or skip it: Tending players keep the emptied map and earn a stipend.

Gather spots have per-season stock (watch the mill blades stop) and a personal cooldown. The scale, banners, everyone's resources: all public. 🛠 dev tools are top right.
